# Privileged and Sensitive Role Training

## Who must complete this

Assign this training to people with administrative access or security, finance, privacy, or people-operations duties. Complete it within 30 days after starting a covered role or taking on covered duties.

## Required practices

- Use separate administrative access where practical and use it only for administrative work.
- Grant, use, and retain the minimum access needed for the assigned duty.
- Require the documented approval and second review that applies to sensitive actions.
- Verify unusual payment, identity, access, data, or account-change requests through an independent channel.
- Keep Confidential and Restricted records in approved systems and share only the minimum needed.
- Do not bypass logging, approval, retention, or monitoring controls.
- Record material decisions and preserve the evidence needed for later review.
- Escalate suspected fraud, privacy, access, or security events immediately through an approved route.

## Completion

Review the policies that apply to the assigned role with this material. Record an attestation tied to the Git revisions reviewed. If a person's role does not meet the audience above, record that decision instead of a completion.
