# Anti-Bribery Training for Higher-Risk Roles

## Who must complete this

Assign this training when a role handles sales, procurement, payments, gifts or hospitality, government interaction, higher-risk locations, or third parties acting for the company. Complete it within 30 days after starting a covered role or taking on covered duties.

## Required practices

- Never offer, request, accept, authorize, or conceal an improper payment or anything of value.
- Apply the policy to cash, gifts, travel, meals, jobs, discounts, donations, favors, and payments made through another party.
- Obtain required approval before gifts, hospitality, donations, sponsorships, or engagement of a higher-risk third party.
- Apply added care to government officials and people acting for a public body.
- Record transactions accurately. Do not use false descriptions, side accounts, or undisclosed arrangements.
- Stop and report pressure to bypass review, split payments, use unusual payment routes, or hide the recipient or purpose.
- Report concerns through an approved independent route. Retaliation for a good-faith report is prohibited.

## Completion

Review the Anti-Bribery and Anti-Corruption Policy with this material. Record an attestation tied to the Git revisions reviewed. If the role does not present the risks above, record that decision instead of a completion.
