# Mobile Computing and Communications Policy

## Purpose

{{company_name}} permits mobile and remote work when devices and communications are protected against loss, interception, unauthorized access, and misuse.

## Scope

This policy applies to laptops, phones, tablets, removable media, home networks, messaging tools, and other technology used to access {{company_name}} information or systems.

## Approved devices and services

- Use company-managed devices for confidential or restricted information unless the policy owner approves another arrangement.
- Use only approved storage, messaging, meeting, email, and file-transfer services.
- Do not move company data to personal accounts or unapproved applications.
- Do not disable security agents, management software, logging, encryption, or remote-wipe controls.
- Rooted, jailbroken, or otherwise security-disabled devices may not access company systems.

## Device security

Devices that access company systems must:

- Use full-device encryption.
- Require a password, passcode, or approved biometric control.
- Lock automatically after no more than 15 minutes of inactivity.
- Use supported operating systems and security updates.
- Install security updates within the remediation targets in the Information Security Policy unless testing or compatibility requires a documented exception.
- Run approved malware protection when available for the platform.
- Permit remote lock or wipe when technically supported.

Passwords and recovery codes must not be stored in unsecured notes, messages, or browser fields.

## Authentication and access

- Use multi-factor authentication for remote access and other systems that support it.
- Do not share accounts or authentication factors.
- Connect through approved secure access methods.
- Access only the information needed for assigned work.
- Remove local copies of confidential information when the work no longer requires them.

## Networks and communications

- Protect home wireless networks with current encryption and a non-default administrator password.
- Avoid transmitting confidential information over public networks. When public access is unavoidable, use an approved encrypted connection.
- Verify recipients before sending confidential information.
- Use approved encryption for files sent outside controlled company systems.
- Do not discuss confidential matters where unauthorized people can overhear them.

## Physical protection

- Keep devices under personal control or secured in a locked location.
- Do not leave devices visible in an unattended vehicle.
- Use privacy screens where shoulder surfing is likely.
- Keep authentication devices separate from an unattended computer when practical.

## Personal devices

Use of a personal device requires written approval. The user must permit the security controls needed to protect company information and must separate company data from personal data where the platform supports it.

{{company_name}} may remove company accounts and data from an approved personal device when access ends, a device is lost, or a security event requires it.

The policy owner periodically reviews authorized mobile devices, personal-device approvals, and remote-access methods. Unused or unsupported connections must be removed.

## Loss, theft, or compromise

Report a lost, stolen, or suspected compromised device immediately to {{policy_owner_name}} at {{security_contact_email}}. Do not delay reporting while attempting to recover or investigate the device.

## Return and disposal

When employment, a contract, or device use ends, return company equipment and remove company data from any approved personal device. Devices and media must be wiped or destroyed using an approved method before reuse or disposal.

## Review

The policy owner reviews this policy at least annually and after a material change in remote-access technology or risk.
