# Clear Desk and Clear Screen Policy

## Purpose

{{company_name}} protects confidential information from accidental exposure, theft, and unauthorized access in offices, shared spaces, and remote work locations.

## Scope

This policy applies to employees and contractors who handle company, customer, workforce, or vendor information.

## Clear desk requirements

When a workspace is unattended:

- Store confidential papers and removable media in a locked or access-controlled location.
- Remove passwords, access codes, keys, badges, and authentication devices from view.
- Do not leave payment information, identity documents, contracts, customer lists, or personnel records exposed.
- Retrieve sensitive print jobs immediately.
- Dispose of sensitive paper in an approved secure-destruction container.
- Return shared meeting rooms and work areas to a clean state.
- Secure portable equipment with a physical lock or security cable where practical.

At the end of the workday, secure confidential material and company equipment.

## Clear screen requirements

- Lock the screen whenever leaving a device unattended.
- Configure automatic screen locking after no more than 15 minutes of inactivity.
- Position screens to reduce unnecessary viewing by visitors or the public.
- Use a privacy screen when confidential information must be viewed in a public or shared location.
- Close files and applications that are no longer needed.
- Do not display passwords, secrets, or recovery codes on monitors or notes.
- Do not leave sensitive files, shortcuts, diagrams, or other information exposed on a shared desktop or display.

## Remote work

Remote workers must choose a workspace where household members, visitors, and the public cannot casually view or access confidential information. Voice and video calls involving confidential matters must be conducted where they cannot be overheard.

## Visitors

Visitors must remain in authorized areas and be supervised where confidential information or systems are present. Hosts are responsible for clearing exposed information before a visitor arrives.

## Lost material or suspected exposure

Report lost papers, devices, badges, removable media, or suspected unauthorized viewing immediately to the current Policy Owner at {{security_contact_email}}.

## Enforcement and review

Failure to follow this policy may lead to access restrictions or disciplinary action. The policy owner reviews this policy at least annually.
